HOW AN ORDER WORKS

From first enquiry to after-care.

We work as a sourcing office in India, helping buyers coordinate requirements with suppliers and processing plants.

For distribution, institutional and retail requirements, we coordinate product specifications and the key steps of the order with the relevant parties.

Enquiry and pricing

Share species, size, packing and quantity requirements. We discuss sourcing information and pricing.

Plant selection and negotiation

Identify suitable supply options and discuss commercial terms.

Quality checks

Coordinate checks against agreed product specifications before and during processing.

Packing and stuffing

Confirm packing requirements and coordinate container stuffing.

Loading survey

Coordinate container loading checks so the shipment follows agreed requirements.

Shipment and after-care

Follow up on shipment coordination and remain available after cargo has sailed.
